## 1. Executive Summary & Epistemological Framework

Combustion research datasets from microgravity flight campaigns (e.g., ISS CIR, MSG, Cygnus Saffire) frequently package raw physical measurements together with post-processed derived quantities, numerical kinetics simulations, and theoretical equilibrium limits within the same spreadsheet or table. 

Conflating numerical simulation predictions with empirical physical measurements introduces catastrophic model bias: an AI or statistical model trained on numerical simulations merely learns the prior assumptions of the numerical solver (e.g., transport approximations, reduced kinetic mechanisms, quasi-steady assumptions), rather than true microgravity physics.

To ensure absolute scientific integrity in FLARE's analysis engine, all acquired tabular columns are categorized into five mutually exclusive epistemological classes:

1. **Experimental Measurement (EM)**: Direct physical transducer signals or sensor readouts recorded during the experiment (e.g., optical tracking coordinates, radiometer photodiode voltages, thermocouple EMFs, spectrometer radiant emissions, chamber pressure transducer outputs).
2. **Numerical Simulation (NS)**: Data points generated by integrating differential governing equations (e.g., 1D spherical multi-component conservation equations, transient finite-difference Navier-Stokes CFD, CHEMKIN-based kinetic solvers, computed temperature fields).
3. **Derived Quantity (DQ)**: Quantities mathematically computed from experimental measurements via deterministic reduction algorithms (e.g., burning rate constant $K = -d(d^2)/dt$, flame standoff ratio $D_f/D_d$, normalized diameter squared $(d/d_0)^2$).
4. **Reference / Theoretical Value (RT)**: Known initial boundary conditions, calibration constants, chemical stoichiometry, or thermodynamic equilibrium calculations (e.g., adiabatic flame temperature $T_{ad}$, certified gas bottle concentrations, nominal initial sample dimensions).
5. **Unresolved (UN)**: Ambiguous columns where documentation or header notation is insufficient to definitively prove whether values originated from experimental instruments or computational models.

## 5. Architectural Recommendations for Milestone 8 Ingestion Engine

1. **Schema Partitioning**: The PostgreSQL relational database must maintain separate target tables:
   - `core_experimental_measurements`
   - `core_derived_kinetics`
   - `benchmark_numerical_simulations`
   - `theoretical_equilibrium_properties`
2. **Column Ingestion Mapping Rules**: Ingestion scripts for PSI-117 must apply deterministic sub-table column slicing as documented in Section 2 above. Under no circumstance should `PROCI_2019_Oscillatory_Cool_Flame.csv` be mapped into `core_experimental_measurements`.
3. **Data Provenance Tagging**: Every stored numeric value must carry an `attribution_class` enum: `['EXPERIMENTAL_MEASUREMENT', 'NUMERICAL_SIMULATION', 'DERIVED_QUANTITY', 'THEORETICAL_REFERENCE', 'UNRESOLVED']` to preserve strict scientific provenance during ML feature extraction.
