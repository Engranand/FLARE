## Engineering restrictions for a later analytical implementation

- Keep raw strings and source units immutable; add reviewed semantic annotations separately. Preserve the existing parser's exact classifications, even where the FAQ now defines OR.
- Gate at cell and record level. Exclude no source records from the audit, but flag negative CO, unusual final O2, ambiguous `over`, mixed fan/pot sequences and conflicting years from automated numeric use until reviewed. Censored values require an explicit censored-data method; they are not exact limits.
- Date/GMT are temporal context, not a complete clock time. No timestamp should be derived without resolving year and confirming the required time convention.
- Air-display response lag and mixed settings prevent assuming simultaneous stable measurements. No flow velocity can be inferred from a restrictor or potentiometer alone. A voltage conversion is not a voltage-to-airflow calibration.
- Frame counts include post-burn images; gain is not intensity. No flame spread rate, extinction limit, combustion outcome, fire-risk probability or model-training target was established here.
- Compare conditions only after reviewing fuel/geometry, same-sample reuse, airflow history, oxygen calibration and sensor validity. Agreement of two source representations does not independently validate either.

Evidence for these restrictions: FAQ pp. 1–2 paragraphs 13–25; workbook and CSV locations in the [anomaly report](bass_ii_anomaly_investigation.md) and [all-record mapping](bass_ii_test_matrix_mapping.md).
